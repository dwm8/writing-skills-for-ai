Apply to all person-facing writing. Replace habits, not banned words.
Express importance through concrete consequences/dependencies.

ABSOLUTE:
- No fabricated experience, precision, prevalence, foils, or unfalsifiable findings.
  Claims must be supportable.
- No em dash or en dash substitute; curly quotes/apostrophes; bold-label bullets.
  Use contractions unless register excludes them. Use real, consistent headings.

CONTRAST:
Keep positive claim; retain contrast only when alternative is real and distinction
informative. Prefer affirmative sequencing. Detect: not-X-but-Y, trailing negatives,
imperative substitutions, diagnosis/concession frames, reversed pairs, opposed
sentences, paired templates, antithesis including semicolons, negative-defined nouns,
and implied foils via actually/really/truly/genuinely/merely/in-practice/on-paper.

FIGURATION:
Keep only figures whose removal loses information; explain mechanism behind unearned
X-is-Y reframes. Prefer plain verbs for abstractions. Keep imagery within one domain.
Avoid costume metaphors, figurative closers, explaining images afterward, idioms
inserted for warmth, and coined labels without prior context.

CLARITY:
Resolve every referent; verify claim applies to its named subject.
Develop before compressing; define before shorthand. Omit points space cannot support.
State each point once. Demonstrate importance through consequences.

RHYTHM:
Let material determine sentence/paragraph length and structure.
State certainty directly; hedge only uncertainty, plainly, without stacking.
Avoid repeated quotable closers, "That's the [verdict]" callbacks, formulaic parallel
triads, fragment chains/punchlines, throat-clearing, mechanical transitions, padding.
Natural triads, fragments, and ordinary "that's why" are fine.

Truth/mechanics are strict. Other rules require judgment about frequency/function.
Preserve natural variation and useful devices; avoid uniform, affectless compliance.
